# Upet legal publication checklist

No policy may be marked approved until every applicable item below is verified by the operator and reviewed by a qualified adviser where needed.

## Operator

- [ ] Legal operator name confirmed.
- [ ] Trading/company status confirmed.
- [ ] Registered or service address confirmed where publication is required.
- [ ] Official legal and privacy contact addresses confirmed.
- [ ] Applicable jurisdictions and mandatory consumer rights reviewed.

## Users and services

- [ ] Eligibility and age approach confirmed.
- [ ] Account creation and termination processes documented.
- [ ] Moderation, restriction and appeal processes documented.
- [ ] All live profile, marketplace, messaging and support features mapped accurately.
- [ ] Paid, premium and marketplace roles described only where actually operational.

## Privacy and cookies

- [ ] Actual personal-data inventory completed from code and operations.
- [ ] Purpose and lawful basis recorded for each use.
- [ ] Firebase, Vercel and every other real processor recorded.
- [ ] International transfer safeguards checked.
- [ ] Retention criteria and deletion workflows approved.
- [ ] User-rights and ICO complaint routes verified.
- [ ] Children’s data and the UK Children’s Code assessed.
- [ ] Cookies, localStorage and analytics inventory completed.
- [ ] Consent behaviour implemented for every non-essential tracker where required.

## Marketplace and payments

- [ ] Platform/seller responsibilities confirmed.
- [ ] Payment processor and payment flow confirmed.
- [ ] Fees, payout timing and taxes confirmed.
- [ ] Refund, cancellation and dispute rules confirmed.
- [ ] Prohibited listings and enforcement process approved.
- [ ] No unsupported escrow or buyer-protection promise appears in policy text.

## Publication record

- [ ] Final text reviewed and approved.
- [ ] Policy version assigned.
- [ ] Approving owner recorded.
- [ ] Approval timestamp recorded.
- [ ] Effective date confirmed.
- [ ] Previous version archived and accessible.
- [ ] Firestore document `approved` changed only through the controlled owner release process.
